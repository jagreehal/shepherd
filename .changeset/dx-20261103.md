Reviewers read a line-numbered diff from `shepherd number`, and findings can anchor on removed lines. `shepherd impact` lists lines that still name a renamed or removed identifier, and `shepherd ci-log` indexes a failed job's log. `ci-repair` merges base conflicts it can settle, triage re-reads each thread before resolving it and counts stamp's inline threads with their issues, and the `shepherd` status waits on a person's requested changes. `interdiff` gives each later round a plain diff of what moved. Adds the `git-archaeology` skill.
