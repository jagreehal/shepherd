The gate now ships inside shepherd: `shepherd gate <pr>` gives a verdict, and `.shepherd/gate.yml` holds its settings. One `SHEPHERD_MODEL=provider/model` drives the loop and the gate. Each provider reads its own key, and every other setting lives in config.

`shepherd init` sets up a repository in one command: it picks the model from your key, writes the config and workflows, stores the key as an Actions secret, and lists what to commit. `shepherd review <pr>` starts a review and prints the run's URL. `shepherd status <pr>` names the stage and your next step. While a run works, the PR shows a "shepherd is reviewing" note.

Inline findings link from the summary to their threads, and a finding swarm marks as a repeat joins its open thread. `ci-repair` checks out the PR's head and follows `fixes.mode` before it changes anything. The new `branch-review` skill reviews work in progress against the team's standards. The "Customize Shepherd for your team" guide takes one review rule from checklist to gate enforcement.
