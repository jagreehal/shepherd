Rounds read more of what models write. A worked example written as text keeps its input, expected and actual result in any order, with any separator, bold labels or synonyms such as `Given` and `Got`, and values keep their quotes and punctuation. A finding without a title takes its body's first sentence, and `line` with `line_end` reads as a range. The replay suite adds a 0.13.1 round and checks titles and worked examples.
