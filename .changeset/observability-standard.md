The observability lens grows to thirteen rules that ask whether an on-call engineer could debug a change in production from its telemetry: spans on entry points and outbound calls, trace context across services and queues, errors with their cause, trace-linked structured logs, request identifiers and decisions, no sensitive data, counted drops, bounded names, alertable signals, feature signals, stable telemetry names, safe startup and shutdown, and sampling and redaction changes.
