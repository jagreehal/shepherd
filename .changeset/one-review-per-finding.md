Each finding posts once. Under `fixes.mode: suggest` with no team lenses, the gate's review is the review: the loop runs no second model, posts nothing, and `shepherd ci-review` sets the `shepherd` status from the gate's verdict. Once the gate approves a head, publish resolves the threads that approval answers with the new `shepherd ci-retire`, then sets the status and notifies from the count it takes afterwards. A thread with a person in it stays open. In the agent loop, a finding with an inline thread is one linked line in the summary, and its text lives in the thread.
