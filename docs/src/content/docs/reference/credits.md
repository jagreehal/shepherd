Shepherd copies some skills unchanged and adapts some lenses from other projects. [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](https://github.com/jagreehal/shepherd/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) holds the notice for each one.

## Credited work

Each entry names the work, its author and its licence:

- **Matt Pocock's skills**: `code-review`, `diagnosing-bugs`, `pr`, `retro` and `writing-for-agents` come from [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) by Matt Pocock, under the MIT License. Shepherd copies them unchanged, so they can be refreshed as he improves them. [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](https://github.com/jagreehal/shepherd/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) records the commit and how to update.
- **`pr`'s Summary section**: it credits Dex Horthy's `show-me` in `skills/pr/CREDITS.md`.
- **The `pstack` lens**: it adapts the principle skills of [pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack) by Lauren Tan, under the MIT License, into review rules.
- **The `thermo-nuclear` lens**: it's adapted under the MIT License.
